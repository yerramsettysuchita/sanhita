A drafting report on the circular itself

A master circular is a consolidation. When two earlier circulars told the same party to do the same thing on different timelines, both sentences survive into the consolidated text, ninety pages apart. Reading front to back will not find them. Comparing typed rules will. Every finding below carries both clauses in full and the SHA-256 of each, so anyone can check it against the published PDF without taking this screen's word for it.
