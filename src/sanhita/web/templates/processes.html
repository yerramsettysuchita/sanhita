From the clause to the desk that owes it

The problem statement asks for a requirement to be mapped to the affected intermediary's operational processes. Knowing that a rule binds a stock broker is not that. It has to reach the process, the team, the system that holds the record and the written procedure, because those are the four things somebody needs before they can act on a finding.

How to read this
  • The clause and the required evidence come from the signed rule. The process, team, system and procedure come from the firm, because two firms reading the same certified rule will organise themselves differently and both be right.
  • Bindings are held beside the rulebook rather than inside it. Recording one never alters the bytes a signature covers, so the 183 certifications stay valid however the firm reorganises.
  • The check at the end of each chain is the live result from the deterministic engine, not a stored status.
