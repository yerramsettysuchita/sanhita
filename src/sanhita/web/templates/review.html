What your documents said, and what it means

A margin report does not contain a Sanhita rule id, because it was written years before Sanhita existed. So an upload produces candidates rather than conclusions, and somebody has to say which duty each one discharges. Sanhita will not guess. Two passages being similar is not proof that a duty was performed, and a tool that treats it as proof will eventually tell a firm it is compliant when it is not.

How this works
  • Nothing awaiting review reaches the engine. An unreviewed upload cannot become a compliance conclusion.
  • A document that names a rule outright, which a CSV export usually does, is mapped by the document itself and needs no confirmation.
  • When a candidate becomes evidence it carries its source with it. The document, the page, the row and the words. A gap report cites the clause to the byte, and the evidence on the other side now answers back.
