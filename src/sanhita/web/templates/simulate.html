What would this amendment cost?

Governments call this a regulatory impact assessment. It is normally done by hand, slowly, on the largest changes only, and often not at all, because answering it means holding a whole rulebook in your head. Over a compiled corpus it is a comparison: apply the draft to a copy, run the same analyses that produce every other screen here, and report the difference.
