The view a supervisor has never had

Every other screen here answers how one firm is doing against one rulebook. This one asks how the market is doing against it. That question is only answerable because one compiled corpus is shared: a clause no firm has operationalised, or a clause two firms certified differently, is a signal that does not exist anywhere today.

How to read this
  • Documents are grouped by parse fingerprint rather than by name, so two firms are only compared when they are demonstrably working from the same bytes of the same circular.
  • Coverage figures are the same ones each document's own coverage screen shows, computed by the same code, over a denominator that is calculated independently of the extractor.
  • This screen reads. It never writes, and it cannot certify, reject or amend anything in any document it can see.
  • Nothing here is aggregated across firms that have not been opened on this machine. It is a view of what is present, not an estimate of a market.
